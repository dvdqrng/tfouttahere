About the numbers

Weather uses the 16-day forecast (Open-Meteo) for any trip days it covers, marked Forecast. Days further out use the typical weather for that time of year from 2022–2025 records (ERA5), marked Typical. Daylight is sunrise to sunset for your exact dates. Sunshine is hours of direct sun per day. It reads about 1 h high in cloudy places, so compare places with each other rather than trusting the absolute number.

Fares are rough estimates from distance, season and how many budget airlines fly the route. Open a destination and check the live prices before you plan around them.
