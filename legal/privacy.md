## What this site does with your data

tf outta here has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no ads. Everything below is what remains.

**Hosting.** The site runs on Vercel (Vercel Inc., USA). When you open it, Vercel's servers process your IP address, the page requested, the time and your browser's user agent, to deliver the page and keep the service secure. Logs are kept briefly. Legal basis: legitimate interest in running the site securely (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). Vercel is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

**Your location (optional).** If you press the location button, or allow it on your first visit, your browser asks for permission and then tells the page your approximate position. The page uses it on your device only to find the nearest airport. Your position is never sent to us or anyone else. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR), which you can refuse or withdraw in your browser settings at any time.

**Saved airport.** Your home airport is stored in your browser's local storage, so the site remembers it next time. It never leaves your device. You can clear it by clearing this site's data in your browser.

**Live departures.** When you open the departures tab, the page asks our server for departures from your airport. The server forwards only the three-letter airport code to AeroDataBox (via RapidAPI) to fetch the schedule. No personal data is passed on.

**Map.** The map is Apple Maps (MapKit JS, Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland). To show it, your browser loads the map script and map tiles directly from Apple's servers, which therefore receive your IP address, your browser's user agent and the area of the map you are looking at. Apple says it does not tie this to an Apple account. Legal basis: legitimate interest in showing a usable map (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

**Fonts and other scripts** are served from this site itself. Weather data is built into the page in advance, so no third-party weather service is contacted while you browse.

**Links to flight search sites.** Google Flights and Skyscanner links open those sites, which have their own privacy policies. Nothing is sent to them until you click.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and to object to processing (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Contact the email above. You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one in your federal state.
